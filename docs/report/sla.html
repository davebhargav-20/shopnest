1. What is an SLA?

An SLA says how available a service should be, how fast it recovers, and what is excluded. AWS has its own SLAs for its services; ShopNest sets simpler targets on top of them for a class-project demo.

5. Exclusions

AWS Academy lab expiry or reset; AWS regional outage; the user's own network; resources stopped by the auto-stop after the KeepAliveUntil date; planned maintenance and demos with deploys frozen; misuse of tags or credentials; HTTP-only transport (no encryption in transit between browser and site).

Honesty note: no uptime percentage is claimed. Availability has not been measured over time, because the system had run for only hours at the time of writing.
